What eNDS is

eNDS is software that emulates Nintendo DS hardware on iPhone and iPad, built on the melonDS emulation core. It runs game files you supply and are entitled to use. No games, proprietary Nintendo BIOS or firmware, or other Nintendo content are included. The core uses the open-source FreeBIOS replacement and generated firmware, so importing Nintendo BIOS files is optional.
